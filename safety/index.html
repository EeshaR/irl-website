Safety Center

United States only — IRL supplements common sense offline, it never replaces emergencies or professional help.

Emergencies come first

If anyone is injured, threatened, intoxicated dangerously, exhibiting weapons, harming minors—or if you hesitate whether it qualifies—invoke local emergency services immediately. Afterwards you may additionally inform us (admin@irlapp.org) with non-urgent follow-up specifics we can correlate with moderation logs.

In-app safeguards (by design)

  • Venue-bounded discovery: presence features aim to correlate with physically sharing a venue context—not roaming an entire metropolitan map.
  • Verification gating: discoverability ordinarily requires satisfying identity checkpoints described inside the app.
  • Anonymity before mutual interest: identity surfaces escalate only along reciprocity pathways defined product-side.
  • Structured coordination: instead of unrestricted chat loops, cues stay intentionally narrow encouraging face-to-face resolution.
  • Automatic session endings: discoverability intends to lapse when leaving a venue perimeter or after maximum windows—see in-app explanatory copy.

Blocking

Use block tools inside the product when available to stop further matching or signal visibility consistent with implementation. Blocking may not retroactively erase prior lawful server logs used for safety investigations.

Reporting users or behavior

Submit in-app reports including relevant category, narrative, approximate local time, venue name. Supplement with email to admin@irlapp.org if media attachments help (redact others' sensitive data when possible). We triage severity: imminent harm vectors jump ahead; complex cases may require multi-day review especially when third-party identity checks are involved.

Moderation approach

Humans plus automated assisting classifiers prioritize proportionality—confirming spoofing versus misunderstanding, escalating patterns across accounts/devices, escalating law enforcement liaison when warranted. Repeat low-grade friction may accumulate enforcement even if singular incidents seemed minor standalone.

Sensitive imagery & illegal content

Child sexual abuse material mandates reporting pipelines—never re-forward illegal images; supply descriptors only unless law enforcement directs otherwise aligned with mandates. Similarly credible violent threats—we treat seriously and may preemptively suspend pending investigation communicating minimally to avoid tipping harassers dangerously.

Doxxing & off-platform escalation

Using discovered identity to bombard alternate channels unsolicited violates guidelines—tell us patterns connecting on-platform facilitation to harassment elsewhere when evidence exists.

Venues can help offline

Quietly engaging staff separates parties in physical space faster than asynchronous digital review sometimes—coordinate practical safety first always.

Ongoing commitments

Transparency improves iteratively—we publish foundational policies publicly (Privacy, Terms, Guidelines) and revise as product realism shifts.
