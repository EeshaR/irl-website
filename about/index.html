About IRL

Real-world discovery, bounded to the room you're in.

Mission

IRL is for people who are already physically present at the same venue and want a low-friction, consent-based path from “maybe I'd say hi” to an actual introduction. Two people privately signal interest first; only when it's mutual do they see each other's full profile—and the product pushes you toward closing the loop in person rather than staying on your phone.

What IRL is not

IRL is not a messaging app. It is not a city-wide swipe feed. It does not prioritize screen time—it prioritizes clarity, boundaries, and a short session: open the app at the venue, opt in thoughtfully, put the phone down.

Privacy and safety posture

Anonymity before mutual consent, identity verification gated before discoverability (as described in the app), blocking and reporting workflows, scoped location use tied to venues, and clear policies for moderation and account deletion—all of these are central to how IRL is designed.
