IRL

Meet people who are physically at the same venue as you—café, bar, coworking space, or event—with a mutual “yes” before anyone's identity is shared. Built for calm, consent-based introductions, not endless scrolling or in-app messaging.

How it works

IRL focuses on one place at a time. When you are inside a supported venue, you can turn on discoverability. Other people there can do the same. Until both of you mutually opt in toward each other, profiles stay anonymous: you see vibe-style signals, not names or photos. There is intentionally no chat in the product—communication is oriented toward coordinating briefly in structured ways and saying hello in real life.

Location is used to confirm you are at a venue (and typically within a short distance consistent with still being at that spot), to support safety features like auto-ending discoverability when you leave, and to keep discovery scoped to the place you share—not across a city or a feed of strangers.

We take verification, reporting, blocking, and account deletion seriously. For any question about the service, moderation, privacy, or your data—contact us at one address: admin@irlapp.org.
