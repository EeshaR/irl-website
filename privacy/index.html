Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how IRL (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use the IRL mobile application, related websites at irlapp.org, and associated services (together, the “Service”). By using the Service, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Service.

Questions or requests about privacy or your data: admin@irlapp.org.

1. Information we collect

Account and authentication data

We collect information needed to create and secure your account, which may include your phone number or email address, authentication tokens, and basic account metadata (such as timestamps related to signup and login). Depending on implementation, third-party authentication providers may also process certain identifiers on our behalf according to their own policies.

Profile information you choose to provide

You may provide profile content such as your first name or display name, photos, written prompts (for example interests, hometown, languages, intentions for using IRL), and similar fields. Photos and detailed profile fields are oriented toward being shown after a mutual connection in the product when that flow applies; anonymous or summarized signals may be derived for pre-connection discovery as described within the app.

Identity verification data

Before you can become discoverable in venues, we may require identity verification in line with posted product rules. That can include scans or images from a government-issued ID, extracted fields such as legal name and date of birth used for eligibility (for example confirming you meet the minimum age requirement), and selfie / liveness imagery used to reduce fraud and impersonation. We use this category of data for safety, eligibility, and compliance—not for unrelated marketing—and we retain it only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes unless a longer period is required by law.

Location information (critical for IRL)

IRL relies on precise or approximate location to operate. We collect location from your device (for example GPS and related signals) while you grant permission so we can determine whether you are at or near a supported venue, scope discovery to users who share that venue context, enforce safety rules such as automatically turning off discoverability when you leave a venue, enforce time limits tied to participation at a venue, and reduce misuse (for example helping ensure discovery is grounded in real-world presence rather than distant browsing).

Depending on permissions and OS behavior, limited location signals may continue to be used in the background consistent with stated product behavior (for instance to detect exiting a venue). You may be able to limit certain permissions via device settings; if you withhold location permission, core venue discovery features may not function.

In-app interactions and coordination signals

IRL does not provide open-ended messaging between strangers. However, we process data about your interactions with the Service, which may include opt-in/opt-out choices, gestures such as waving or similar signals defined in-app, mutual connection events, match state, structured coordination choices (such as presets for where to meet inside a venue or whether you indicate you are heading over), moderation-related flags, blocked accounts, reports you submit about other users or content, and similar activity records needed for safety, auditing, ranking, troubleshooting, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.

Contacts (if enabled)

Where the product implements contact-based features (for example to reduce unintended overlap with people in your address book), we may access or process contacts only if you grant permission, and solely for disclosed purposes consistent with product settings.

Device and technical data

We collect diagnostics and technical information such as device type, OS version, app version, language, IP address (which may approximate general location), push notification tokens where used, logs related to crashes or reliability, identifiers used for analytics or attribution (which may include advertising identifiers where permitted), and timestamps of events inside the Service.

Cookies and similar technologies (website)

When you visit irlapp.org we may use typical web technologies consistent with hosting and analytics tooling. Preferences can often be adjusted through browser settings or any cookie banner tooling we adopt.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide core functionality: authentication, profiles, discovery scoped to venues, mutual-consent workflows, coordination signals permitted by the product, notifications where enabled, and product integrity.
  • Operate location-based safeguards: verifying venue participation, enforcing auto-off behaviors, and aligning matches with stated presence rules.
  • Safety and trust: verifying accounts, enforcing policies, detecting abuse, investigating reports, restricting or removing accounts when appropriate, preventing fraud, protecting users and the public, and meeting legal obligations.
  • Improve the Service: understanding usage patterns, diagnosing errors, refining ranking and relevance, evaluating features (including through analytics and experimentation).
  • Communicate with you: transactional messages, administrative notices about terms or privacy, responding to inquiries sent to admin@irlapp.org, service-related announcements when permitted.
  • Legal bases (where applicable such as GDPR): performance of contract, legitimate interests balanced against your rights, consent where sought explicitly, legal obligation where required.

3. Automated processing

We may apply automated techniques (including AI-assisted summaries or derived labels) to help generate anonymous-facing descriptions or classify content for moderation, relevance, safety, or product presentation. Automated decisions may inform enforcement with human review depending on severity and practicality.

4. How we share information

We share information only as needed in these categories:

  • Other users: per product rules—for example anonymity before mutual opt-in versus richer profile disclosure after mutual connection as implemented in-app.
  • Service providers: cloud hosting, database storage, verification vendors, analytics, notifications, fraud prevention, email delivery—bound by contractual confidentiality and security expectations.
  • Authorities and parties when required: to comply with law, subpoena, court order, or governmental request where we deem disclosure legally required or necessary to protect safety, rights, or property.
  • Corporate transactions: in connection with a merger, financing, restructuring, acquisition, dissolution, asset sale, due diligence subject to safeguards where applicable.
  • Aggregated or de-identified data: that cannot reasonably identify you.

5. Third-party services

The Service may integrate third-party SDKs or links (authentication, maps/places vendors, verification, analytics, etc.). Their processing is governed by their policies too. Avoid sharing sensitive information into third-party features beyond what we direct.

6. Retention

We keep information while your account remains active or as needed to operate the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain safety telemetry. Verification materials may follow a distinct retention posture described in-flow or amended notices. Backup systems may briefly retain remnants after deletion; we purge or overwrite over time consistent with ordinary IT practice.

7. Security

We implement technical and organizational measures appropriate to risk. No system is perfectly secure—protect your credentials and device access.

8. Regional rights

Depending where you live, privacy laws may give you rights to access, correct, export, restrict, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent without affecting lawful prior processing; to lodge complaints with regulators; or to nominate limits on sale/share of personal data where those concepts apply. Email admin@irlapp.org to exercise requests. You may appeal certain decisions within reasonable timeframes as required locally.

9. Children

The Service is not for users under the minimum eligible age enforced in-product (typically 18+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors below that cutoff. Contact us if you believe otherwise.

10. International transfers

We may process information in countries other than yours. Where required we implement appropriate safeguards such as contractual clauses acknowledged by regulators to govern cross-border transfers.

11. Account deletion

You may request deletion in-app following current instructions (Delete account) or email admin@irlapp.org from your registered address describing the request. Completed deletion removes or de-identifies personal data except where retention is legally required or permitted (for instance limited security logs tied to enforcing terms).

12. Changes

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes may be signaled through in-app prompts, email notice where appropriate, or an updated posting date above. Continuing to use the Service after changes constitutes acceptance unless law requires differently.
